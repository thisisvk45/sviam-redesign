About

We are building the interview, not the résumé filter.

Maya is an AI technical interviewer. It listens to your reasoning, watches you write real code, and asks the follow-up a senior engineer would. The point is not a score. It is honest evidence a person can judge.

It shows its work.
Not just a number.

Most hiring tools filter résumés before a person reads them, or hand back a single black-box score and ask you to trust it. Both throw away the thing that matters in an interview: how someone reasons through a problem. Maya is built the other way around.

Follows your reasoning

Maya works alongside the code you write and interrupts when it matters, tracking how you break a problem down and handle the hard cases.

Produces evidence

It hands people the record: the code you wrote, how you talked through it, and where you were strong or unsure. Not one opaque grade.

Leaves the call to people

Maya does not make the hiring decision. People do. Its job is to make that decision better informed, not to replace it.
