Questions

What is included in Free?

Two full interviews with Maya, available once per account for the lifetime of the account. Each one comes with a scorecard showing your strengths and gaps. This is a one-time allowance, not a monthly reset.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment of ₹499 for 10 full interviews within a 30-day access period. It does not auto-renew, and nothing is charged again after your access period ends.

Do unused interviews reset each month?

No. The 10 Pro interviews are available during your 30-day access period. There is no monthly reset, and unused interviews do not roll over.

How does checkout work?

Nothing is charged on this page. Checkout runs inside the product after you sign in, and Pro is unlocked only once your payment is verified.

Can a company use SViam?

Yes, through a limited pilot. The pilot gives you your first 5 candidate interviews free, with role setup, invite links, reasoning transcripts, code replay and a scorecard per candidate. Reach out through the companies page to request access.

Who runs SViam?

SViam is operated by SVIAM AI Private Limited. For any question about pricing or your account, email vikas.kumar@sviam.in.
