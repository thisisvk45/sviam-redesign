Questions

The honest answers to what people ask most, about practice, plans, and where the hiring product stands today.

Is SViam live?

Practice is live today. You solve coding questions with Maya, an AI interviewer, and receive an honest scorecard after your session. The employer product is in a limited pilot, not yet self-serve.

What is included in Free?

Two introductory interviews, available once per account, with a standard scorecard. The free allowance is lifetime and does not reset each month.

What does Pro cost and how long does it last?

Pro is ₹499, a one-time payment for 10 interviews within 30 days. It does not renew automatically, and unused interviews do not roll over.

What kinds of questions can I practise?

Arrays and hashing, stacks, intervals, graphs, trees, binary search, and design, across Easy, Medium, and Hard. Examples include Two Sum, Valid Parentheses, LRU Cache, Course Schedule, and Word Ladder.

Can my company use SViam?

The employer product is in a pilot phase. Contact us through the hiring pilot to discuss your requirements. It is not yet available as a self-serve company plan.

How do I get help or reach you?

Email vikas.kumar@sviam.in. If you are reporting an interview issue, include the session link and what happened.
